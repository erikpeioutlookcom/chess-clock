chess-clock

A clean, precise chess clock with every major time control, built for phones and works offline.

Chess Clock is a fast, professional-grade chess clock that runs in any browser and installs as an app on your phone. Tap your side after each move. Choose from presets like Bullet, Blitz, Rapid and Classical, or set a custom time with Fischer, Bronstein or simple delay, handicap time, and bonus time after a set move. It adds precise tenths-of-a-second timing, undo, face-to-face mode for playing across a phone, light and dark themes, sound alerts, and offline use once installed.
